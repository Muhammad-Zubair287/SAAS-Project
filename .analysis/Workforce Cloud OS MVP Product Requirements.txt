1. Purpose of This Document
This Product Requirements Document defines the detailed product behaviour required for the first
commercially usable version of Workforce Cloud OS.
The document translates the Business Requirements Document into:
Product modules.
Features and subfeatures.
User stories.
Functional requirements.
Business rules.
User workflows.
Data validations.
Permissions.
Notifications.
Reports.
Dependencies.
Acceptance criteria.
MVP boundaries.
The primary purpose is to ensure that product, design, engineering, testing and business teams share
one interpretation of what must be delivered.
The system is based on the approved concept of a connected, multi-tenant workforce operating
environment combining employee data, HR processes, attendance, payroll, workflows, reporting and
self-service capabilities.

2. Product Summary
Workforce Cloud OS will provide one workforce system of record for organisations that currently
manage employees through disconnected HR applications, biometric systems, payroll spreadsheets,
emails, paper forms and separate reporting tools.
The MVP will focus on the most frequent and commercially valuable workforce processes:
Tenant and company setup.
User authentication.
Role-based access.
Organisation structures.
Employee records.
Employee onboarding.
Attendance.
Shifts and rosters.
Leave.
Approval workflows.
Payroll.
Employee self-service.
Manager self-service.
Notifications.
Reports and exports.
Data migration and integrations.
Subscription and module entitlements.
Audit, privacy and security controls.
The product must support a connected operating model:
Employee record → attendance → leave → payroll → approvals → employee self-service
→ management reporting.
3. Product Vision
Workforce Cloud OS will become the secure operating layer through which
organisations manage employees, attendance, leave, payroll, policies, approvals
and workforce information.
The MVP must create a stable foundation for future modules such as TaskOps, assets, expenses,
benefits, performance, compliance automation and AI insights.
The approved product roadmap places Core HR, attendance, leave and payroll in Phase One, followed
by TaskOps, assets, benefits and intelligence in later phases.

4. Product Goals
PG-001: Establish a Single Employee Record
Every employee shall have one authoritative profile used across attendance, leave, payroll, documents,
approvals and reporting.
PG-002: Digitise High-Frequency Workforce Processes
The platform shall replace manual attendance, leave, employee-record and payroll processes with
digital workflows.
PG-003: Connect Attendance and Payroll
Approved attendance, leave and overtime information shall feed payroll calculations.
PG-004: Enable Employee Self-Service
Employees shall be able to access routine information and submit common requests without depending
on HR.
PG-005: Enable Manager Self-Service
Managers shall be able to view their teams and process authorised workforce requests.
PG-006: Support Tenant Configuration
Each customer shall be able to configure its own organisation structure, policies, workflows, salary
components and branding.
PG-007: Maintain Auditability
Critical employee, attendance, payroll and approval actions shall remain traceable.
PG-008: Support Commercial SaaS Operations
The platform shall support customer tenants, plans, seat limits, feature entitlements and usage
controls.
PG-009: Support Pakistan Launch Requirements
The MVP shall support Pakistan-focused payroll configuration, PKR, local holidays, biometric integration
foundations and English-language operation, with Urdu localisation prepared for controlled rollout.

PG-010: Provide an Expandable Architecture
The product shall allow future modules to use the same employee, organisation, workflow, notification
and audit foundations.
5. MVP Non-Goals
The MVP will not attempt to provide:
Complete global payroll processing.
Full accounting or general ledger capabilities.
Full recruitment and applicant tracking.
Advanced learning management.
Advanced project portfolio management.
AI-based termination or promotion decisions.
Predictive attrition models.
Full asset lifecycle management.
Advanced employee benefits management.
Full expense reimbursement automation.
Automated government tax filing.
Native support for every biometric-device vendor .
Separate custom codebases for individual customers.
Complex gamification.
Advanced compensation planning.
Full workforce scheduling optimisation.
These may be introduced in later product phases.
6. Target Customer Profile
The MVP shall primarily serve organisations with:
50–1,000 employees.
One or more offices, branches or operating locations.
Manual or biometric attendance.
Spreadsheet-based or disconnected payroll.
Formal leave and approval requirements.
HR or finance staff responsible for payroll.
A requirement for employee self-service.
A need for audit-ready workforce records.
Priority sectors include:
Technology and software.
Business-process outsourcing.
Healthcare.
Retail.

Audit record preserves original and revised values.

40. Final Product Recommendation
Workforce Cloud OS should enter the market with a focused and dependable MVP rather than
attempting to launch every planned workforce module simultaneously.
The first product must perform six things exceptionally well:
Maintain accurate employee records.
Record and calculate attendance.
Manage shifts and leave.
Calculate and approve payroll.
provide employee and manager self-service.
Maintain security and auditability.
Once these foundations have been validated with real customers and parallel payroll cycles, the
platform can expand into TaskOps, assets, finance, benefits, compliance automation and AI.
The central product principle shall remain:
One employee identity, one organisation structure, one policy foundation, one
approval framework and one connected workforce operating environment.
This PRD provides the detailed product baseline required to begin user-flow design, wireframing, data
modelling, solution architecture, backlog creation, engineering estimation and design-partner
implementation.
